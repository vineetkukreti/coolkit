## PROMPT:

I'm building CoolKit — a self-hosted, open-source job hunting automation platform. It already has: 120+ job source scraping, auto-apply engine, cold email outreach with A/B testing, IMAP reply detection, pipeline tracker (kanban), LinkedIn post paste-and-send workflow, company signals detection, referral detection, skills intelligence, and a daily orchestrator.

I need you to do DEEP competitive research. Search the web extensively for each section. Be thorough — I want data, not opinions.

### SECTION 1: Competitor Deep Dives (Search each one)

For EACH of these tools, search their website, ProductHunt page, TrustPilot reviews, Reddit discussions, and any review articles. Report: features, pricing, USP, what users love, what users hate.

1. **Sonara.ai** — auto-apply
2. **LazyApply** — multi-platform auto-fill
3. **Simplify.jobs** — free Chrome extension + autofill
4. **Teal HQ** — resume builder + tracker (acquired Ramped)
5. **Huntr** — best-in-class job tracker
6. **JobRight.ai** — AI matching + insider connections
7. **LoopCV** — auto-apply + resume A/B testing
8. **Scale.jobs** — human assistants apply for you
9. **Careerflow** — LinkedIn optimizer + networking CRM
10. **FinalRound AI** — mock interview + real-time copilot
11. **Career-Ops by Santiago Fernandez** — multi-agent scoring system (GitHub)
12. **AIHawk / Jobs_Applier_AI_Agent** — mass LinkedIn auto-apply (GitHub, archived)
13. **JustAJobApp / jobseeker-analytics** — Gmail auto-tracker (GitHub)
14. **CareerSync** — zero-storage Gmail tracking (GitHub)

### SECTION 2: Cold Outreach Tools Used by Job Seekers

Search how job seekers (not salespeople) use these tools. What response rates do they get? What templates work?

1. **Lemlist** — multichannel sequences
2. **Instantly.ai** — unlimited cold email
3. **Mixmax** — Gmail-native tracking
4. **Mailshake** — cold email sequences
5. **Dripify** — LinkedIn drip campaigns
6. **PhantomBuster** — LinkedIn data extraction
7. **Expandi** — behavioral LinkedIn automation

### SECTION 3: Feature Innovation Research

Search ProductHunt, GitHub trending, Hacker News "Show HN", and tech blogs for:

1. Ghost job detection tools (GhostBust.us, GhostJobs.io, etc.)
2. AI resume tailoring tools and their ATS scoring methods
3. Interview question prediction features
4. Salary negotiation AI tools
5. Referral/warm intro automation
6. Job search funnel analytics approaches
7. Company culture intelligence aggregation
8. Reverse recruiting services and their pricing
9. Browser extensions for job saving/tracking
10. Skill gap analysis tools with learning paths

### SECTION 4: Pain Points Deep Dive

Search Reddit (r/jobs, r/cscareerquestions, r/recruitinghell, r/jobsearchhacks), Hacker News, Twitter/X, and Blind for:

1. What are the top 10 frustrations job seekers post about most?
2. What "I wish there was a tool that..." requests appear most often?
3. What DIY tools/spreadsheets/scripts do people build themselves?
4. What makes job seekers quit using auto-apply tools?
5. What's the ghosting problem like in 2026 and what do people want done about it?
6. What cold email strategies do job seekers report actually working?
7. What follow-up cadences get results?

### SECTION 5: Market Data & Statistics

Search for the latest 2025-2026 data on:

1. Average applications to get one interview / one offer
2. ATS rejection rates (is the "75% auto-rejected" stat real?)
3. Cold email response rates for job seekers vs salespeople
4. LinkedIn InMail vs cold email effectiveness
5. Referral hire rates vs cold application rates
6. Hidden job market size (% never posted)
7. Ghost job prevalence statistics
8. Job seeker mental health impact data
9. Tech layoff numbers Q1-Q2 2026
10. AI usage in hiring (both sides)

### SECTION 6: Feature Prioritization

Based on ALL the research above, create a prioritized feature roadmap for CoolKit:

**Format each feature as:**
- Name
- One-line description
- Why it matters (data-backed)
- What competitors charge for it
- Build effort (Low/Medium/High)
- Impact score (1-10)
- Uniqueness score (1-10 — how many competitors have it?)

Rank by: (Impact * Uniqueness) / Effort

### OUTPUT FORMAT

Please organize everything into a clean, structured report with:
- Table of contents
- Data tables where appropriate
- Source URLs for every statistic
- A final "Top 5 Features to Build Next" recommendation with reasoning

Make it comprehensive enough to be a strategy document I can reference for months.
